ORIGINAL PROJECT DESCRIPTION

AI-enabled multi-organ biological-age estimation, health-risk analysis and IoT-based personalized health intelligence system

Bio-Revive 360° is an AI-powered, hardware-integrated system that detects the biological age of every major organ and corrects lifestyle-induced degeneration caused by excessive gadget use, junk food, stress, and physical inactivity.

Academic AI and data-analytics prototype that scans or ingests health reports, extracts and structures relevant information, analyses selected parameters across multiple domains, and converts results into clear visual and textual insights for user awareness and education.
